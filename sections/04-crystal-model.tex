\section{The \crystal Model}
\label{sec:model}

\subsection{Scope and Rated Attribute}
\label{sec:model-scope}
\crystal models the \emph{network-attributable} component of \qoe for
\vpcc streaming over reliable \quic: the degradation in perceived
temporal smoothness caused by delay and loss, relative to an
impairment-free presentation of the same content. It does not model
compression fidelity, which is fixed per sequence and reported through
\pcqm as a stimulus descriptor, and it does not model overall MOS as a
combination of fidelity and smoothness. The rated attribute in the
subjective study is smoothness of playback.

\subsection{Model Predictions}
\label{sec:model-predictions}
Because $\tlf$ is derived rather than fitted, the model commits to
three predictions before any rating is collected, and the evaluation of
Section~\ref{sec:results} is organised around them.

\emph{Latency and loss interact.} Both impairments enter $\tlf$ jointly
through the slack $S$ and the recovery cost $R$, so their effects on
\mos cannot be additive as Eq.~\eqref{eq:udora} assumes. Across the
four slices of Section~\ref{sec:design-network} the model predicts
$A \approx B \approx C \gg D$: either impairment alone is absorbed by
the slack, and only their combination exhausts it.

\emph{Delivery-unit structure, not loss magnitude, governs
resilience.} For fixed delay and loss, $\tlf$ depends on the \gof size
$G$ through both the number of packets at risk per unit and the slack
available to recover them. \todo{State the direction Layer~1 predicts
for \gls{ldp} \gof{}=4 versus \gof{}=16.}

\emph{One perceptual parameter generalises.} A single $\alpha$ fitted
on one subset of conditions predicts held-out conditions without
refitting, at a held-out \gls{plcc} exceeding the 0.57--0.66 reported
for the five-coefficient regression of Eq.~\eqref{eq:udora}.
